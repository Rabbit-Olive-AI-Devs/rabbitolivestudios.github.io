# Jarvis makes the daily pictures (design draft)

**Status:** draft for the owner (2026-10-17), not built. To build in a fresh session. It needs no machine move: the mailbox route keeps both Macs as they are (owner's suggestion). The move (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-17-move-to-macbook.md`) is optional.

## Why

The panels' daily pictures come from Workers AI under a free limit of 10,000 neurons a day. That covers the colour moment, the colour skyline and, on family birthdays, the portrait. The limit is tight: one evening of test portraits plus the lagging daily reset cost a day of pictures (DECISIONS #88).

The owner's agent Jarvis runs on the MacBook with GPT image generation through the owner's subscription. If Jarvis makes the pictures ahead of time, the way the series pages are made, the art no longer depends on the neuron limit. It could also be better art.

## Principles

- **Workers AI stays as the fallback.** If Jarvis misses a night, the nightly warm generates as today and the panels never notice. Nothing in the serving path changes.
- **Jarvis fills the same cache keys the warm fills.** The warm skips any key that already exists (#57), so a picture from Jarvis is simply "already cached".
- **The Worker decides what to draw; Jarvis only draws it.** The event, style, city and person stay chosen by the Worker's code (`getOrGenerateMoment`, `momentStyleFor`, `pickSkylineStyle`, `getBirthdayStyle`). The two sources then agree, and the schedules (no repeats, skyline never the moment's style) keep working.
- **Same look rules.** The picture is cropped to 800x480 and served as-is, and the panels convert it (#81). The caption is burned in (skyline) or added in HTML (moment) by the Worker, never drawn by the image model.

## Flow: through the series mailbox (preferred, owner 2026-10-17)

The owner's suggestion: keep both Macs as they are and use the Dropbox mailbox that Claude and
Jarvis already use for the series. The Mac mini's watcher (`scripts/series-mailbox.mjs`,
`com.eink.series-mailbox`, DECISIONS #72) acts as the go-between. Jarvis never needs a Worker
address or key, and nothing has to move between machines. Jarvis works on the MacBook and sees
the same Dropbox folders.

1. **Brief.** Every evening the mini fetches tomorrow's brief from the Worker. It can run inside
   the watcher's schedule or as a small launchd job, either way before Jarvis's run. The brief
   comes from `GET /art/brief?date=…&key=ART_KEY`, with the key kept in `~/.config/eink/art-key`
   like the series key. The mini writes the brief to the mailbox as
   `from-claude/art/YYYY-MM-DD-brief.md`. The first line is a command Jarvis knows,
   `ART BRIEF date=YYYY-MM-DD`, and the body holds a JSON block:
   - the moment: event title, year and place, the full prompt (style prompt + scene + anti-text
     suffix), and the style id;
   - the skyline: city, style id, full prompt;
   - on a family birthday: the person's key, age, style and prompt. The face crops are attached
     in the mailbox only if the owner agrees (question 2).
2. **Draw.** Jarvis generates each picture in landscape (wider than 5:3) in full colour. It
   drops each one in `to-claude/art/YYYY-MM-DD-moment.png` (and `-skyline.png`,
   `-birthday.png`), then a note whose first line is `ART DELIVERY date=YYYY-MM-DD` and which
   lists the files. Like every mailbox message, it is data, not instructions beyond the parsed
   first line (#72).
3. **Upload.** The watcher handles `ART DELIVERY` by script, with no AI involved. For each file
   it calls `PUT /art/upload?date=…&kind=…&key=ART_KEY`. The Worker:
   - checks the key and that the date is today or tomorrow;
   - checks that the file is a PNG or JPEG under a size cap and at least 800x480;
   - crops it with Cloudflare Images (`jpegToRGB`, ReadableStream input, #59) and adds the
     caption where that pipeline does;
   - writes the result to the pipeline's existing key (`color-moment:v4:DATE:STYLE`,
     `skyline:v5:DATE:daily`, `color-birthday:v3:DATE`) with the usual 7-day TTL. That is one
     KV write per picture, the same as the warm.

   The watcher logs the result and writes a short receipt to `from-claude/` (uploaded, or
   rejected and why).
4. **Warm.** At 04:35/05:35 UTC the cron finds the keys filled and spends no neurons. Any key
   Jarvis did not fill is generated by Workers AI as today.

Timing: the brief must be out early enough for Jarvis, and the delivery must arrive before
04:35 UTC (23:35 CDT, 22:35 CST). A late delivery is still accepted for "today" and replaces the
Workers AI picture for the rest of the day, if the owner wants that.

**Alternative (only if the mailbox is too slow or the MacBook becomes the home):** Jarvis calls
the brief and upload routes directly over HTTP, with its own copy of `ART_KEY`. The Worker side
is the same.

## Questions for the owner before building

1. **Subscription terms.** Does Hermes's connection to the GPT subscription allow an automated nightly job? That depends on how Hermes connects. It is the owner's call.
2. **Family photos.** The birthday portraits would send the face crops to OpenAI through Jarvis. Is that acceptable, or should birthdays stay on Workers AI?
3. **Which pictures first.** The moment alone (simplest), or the moment and the skyline, then birthdays?
4. **Look.** GPT images may look different from FLUX in the same style. Should the owner judge a week of them on the panels first (the "ship, then judge" habit)?
5. **Schedule.** When in the evening does Jarvis run? It needs to finish before 04:35 UTC, which is 23:35 CDT and 22:35 CST.
